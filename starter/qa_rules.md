# QA checklist

The QA tab shows every check below; **Mark PASS** is only possible when all of them are ticked, and the strip reel is
only built for a picture with a `PASS <sha1>` line in its `qa.md`. Edit this file to make the checklist your own — keep
the `**A. Title.** text` form (one letter per check).

## Checklist — every answer must be YES
**A. The story reads cold.** Someone who has never seen the series gets the joke: panel 1 sets it up, the last panel pays it off.
**B. On model.** Every character looks like themselves (hair, eyes, outfit, accessories) in every panel.
**C. Reading order.** Panels and speech balloons read in the right order; each balloon points at the right speaker.
**D. Text.** Every word is spelled exactly as in the script; nothing else is written anywhere.
**E. Anatomy.** Two arms, two legs, five-ish fingers or clean mitten hands; no fused bodies or clothes; faces not warped.
**F. Style.** Same line, colour and proportions as your style reference (use Style check to compare).
**G. Clean.** No stray logos, watermarks or brand names; nothing cut off at the edge.
