# Art style

Your look in words. The **Prompt** tab (only shown when Settings → *Where do the pictures come from?* is ChatGPT) builds
a prompt from the header below, the panel layout, your panels and the LOCK sections you tick. Drawing yourself? Use this
file as your own style notes.

## Prompt header (paste first, then layout.txt, then the panels)

> Draw a fan-art comic strip of [CHARACTER A] and [CHARACTER B] from [SERIES]. Portrait 4:5. One image only.
> MATCH THE ART STYLE OF THE ATTACHED STYLE REFERENCE EXACTLY.
> LAYOUT: follow the attached wireframe exactly — the numbers are the reading order; do NOT draw the numbers.
> Simple hand-drawn look: sketchy outlines, flat colours, minimal backgrounds, hand-written lettering.
> Spell every line EXACTLY as written; nothing else written anywhere.

## OUTFIT LOCK
Characters keep their usual outfits unless the script says otherwise. Describe each outfit here.

## ANATOMY LOCK: add this line to EVERY image prompt
Natural limbs and hands, two arms and two legs each, no fused bodies or clothes, faces not warped.
